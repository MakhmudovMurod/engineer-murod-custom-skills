# Uzbek Copy Patterns, Editing Passes, and Synthetic Examples

## 1. Brief Normalization

Convert every request into this table before drafting:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Product | What exactly is being promoted? |
| Audience | Who in Uzbekistan should care now? |
| Pain | What familiar situation creates demand? |
| Promise | What one practical outcome matters most? |
| Mechanism | How does the product produce that outcome? |
| Proof | What can be demonstrated or verified? |
| Terms | Price, deadline, eligibility, geography, restrictions? |
| CTA | What single action should the reader take? |
| Voice | Creator commentary, expert, brand, event, or community? |
| Disclosure | What label is legally/editorially required? |

If the brief offers ten features, rank them by: relevance × uniqueness × proof. Draft around the top one; use at most three supporting details.

## 2. Uzbek Localization Ladder

Do not stop at translating nouns. Localize through four levels:

1. **Language:** Correct Uzbek words and orthography.
2. **Register:** Spoken creator voice rather than official notice.
3. **Situation:** A scene readers in Uzbekistan recognize.
4. **Social tension:** The embarrassment, skepticism, convenience, aspiration, or humor around that scene.

Example:

- Translation only: `Qarzlarni boshqarish uchun avtomatik eslatmalar.`
- Register: `Bot qarzni o‘zi eslatib turadi.`
- Situation: `Har hafta “aka, pulni qachon berasiz?” deb yozib o‘tirmaysiz.`
- Social tension: `Pul o‘zingizniki, lekin so‘rashga baribir siz uyalasiz.`

The fourth level creates the native hook.

## 3. Useful Sentence Functions

### Recognition

- `Tanish holat-a?`
- `Shunaqa paytda ...`
- `Ko‘pchilikda shu muammo bor: ...`
- `Pul o‘zingizniki, lekin ...`

### Honest skepticism

- `Rostini aytsam, ...`
- `Odatda bunaqa va’dalarga ishonmayman.`
- `Qog‘ozda chiroyli. Amalda-chi?`
- `Bu yerda gap reklamadagi chiroyli so‘zlarda emas.`

### Pivot

- `Xullas, ...`
- `Aytgancha, ...`
- `Gap shundaki, ...`
- `Shu muammo uchun ...`

### Demonstration

- `Masalan, ...`
- `Jarayon oddiy: ...`
- `Sizdan kerak bo‘ladigani — ...`
- `Eng qulay joyi — ...`

### Caveat

- `Bitta muhim joyi: ...`
- `Shartlari bilan oldindan tanishib chiqing.`
- `Narx/limit hududga qarab farq qiladi.`
- `Bu [regulated product] bo‘lgani uchun ...`

### Action

- `Shartlarini ko‘rib chiqing: [LINK]`
- `Joyni band qiling: [LINK]`
- `Sinab ko‘ring: [LINK]`
- `Ro‘yxatdan o‘ting: [LINK]`

Do not force these exact phrases. They are functional examples.

## 4. Headline Patterns

### Pain inversion

`[Desired relationship/outcome]ni yo‘qotmoqchimisiz? [Common mistake].`

Use only when the inversion is logical and not cruel.

### Contradiction

`[Common belief]. Lekin muammo [surprising truth]da.`

### Specific scene

`[Moment]da [pain] bo‘lganlar bormi?`

### Honest test

`[Goal] uchun [product]ni sinab ko‘rdim. Eng foydali joyi [result] ekan.`

Only with truthful supplied experience.

### Outcome + constraint

`[Time/effort] ichida [specific outcome] — [audience] uchun.`

Avoid guaranteed transformation.

### Self-aware reveal

`“Hozir reklama chiqadi”, deyapsizlar. Mayli, [playful denial that naturally reveals facts].`

Requires a creator voice and clear disclosure.

## 5. Structure Patterns

### 5.1 Problem-solution native post

```text
[Recognizable problem in one line]

[2–3 sentences showing the awkward or costly reality.]

Xullas, [product] [simple mechanism].

— [scene-based benefit]
— [scene-based benefit]
— [verified term/proof]

[One caveat or condition.]

[CTA]: [LINK]
Hamkorlik asosida
```

### 5.2 First-person walkthrough

```text
[Real need or search]

[What the author tried/found.]

Jarayon shunaqa bo‘ldi:
1. [step]
2. [step]
3. [result]

Menga yoqqan joyi — [specific practical benefit].
[Who it fits / limitation.]

[CTA]: [LINK]
Reklama
```

### 5.3 Direct event/admission card

```text
[Outcome/event] — [audience] uchun.

📅 [date/time]
📍 [place/format]

Nimalar bo‘ladi:
— [useful item]
— [useful item]
— [verified benefit]

[Real deadline or capacity condition.]
[CTA]: [LINK]
Reklama
```

One organizing emoji system is enough. Do not decorate every bullet.

### 5.4 Short reminder

```text
[Deadline/outcome]gacha [time] qoldi.

[One strongest benefit].
[One eligibility/price fact].

[CTA]: [LINK]
Reklama
```

## 7. Three Editing Passes

### Pass A — Translation-smell removal

Search and simplify:

- `mazkur` → name the thing
- `amalga oshirish` → direct verb
- `taqdim etadi` → `beradi`, `bor`, or specific action
- `foydalanish imkoniyatiga ega bo‘lasiz` → `foydalanasiz`
- `sizni ... kutmoqda` → state what happens
- `batafsil ma'lumot olishingiz mumkin` → say what the reader learns
- `yuqori sifatli` → specify the measurable quality

### Pass B — Claim audit

Underline every:

- number;
- deadline;
- comparative (`tezroq`, `arzonroq`);
- superlative;
- guarantee;
- personal claim;
- regulated claim.

Each must come from the user/client/source. Delete or mark placeholders otherwise.

### Pass C — Native-fit audit

Ask:

1. Would a real person in this channel say the first line?
2. Is there a recognizable Uzbek-life scene?
3. Does the product enter after relevance is established?
4. Are benefits visible without marketing adjectives?
5. Is there one CTA?
6. Is sponsorship clear?
7. Could 20% be cut without losing meaning?

## 8. Tone Calibration

### More Troll-like ecosystem energy, without imitation

- sharper social observation;
- one contradiction or self-aware joke;
- specific local scene;
- calm product explanation after the punchline;
- low emoji count.

### More expert/Teacher energy

- newsworthy or educational hook;
- context and mechanism;
- straightforward conclusion;
- less slang, more clarity;
- factual links/proof.

### More reflective/Kunduziy energy

- personal stance;
- audience relationship and self-awareness;
- longer thought before the offer;
- slightly playful vulnerability;
- useful for community, media, and creator products.

Never say the draft is “in [living creator]'s exact style.” Describe it as `conversational Uzbek creator-channel native copy`.

## 9. Compliance Reminders

- **Finance/crypto:** mention risk/eligibility/fees where required; no guaranteed profit.
- **Health:** no diagnosis or cure claims without substantiation; identify professional qualifications.
- **Education:** grants, rankings, employment rates, and admission claims need evidence and conditions.
- **Installments:** show down payment, term, rate, insurance/fees, and individual approval conditions.
- **Giveaways:** include organizer, eligibility, deadline, selection rules, and prize conditions.
- **Scarcity:** use only if objectively true.
- **Testimonials:** real and authorized; never synthesize a customer quote.
